## Description

### Issue Summary
Customer reports being charged twice after subscription renewal.

### Customer Impact
Customer was billed twice and requested an immediate refund.

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Customer renews subscription.
2. Payment is processed.
3. A duplicate charge appears.

### Expected Result
Customer should only be charged once.

### Actual Result
Two successful payment transactions were processed.

### Business Impact
This issue affects customer trust and payment processing.
