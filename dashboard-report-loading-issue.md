# Dashboard Reports Not Loading

## Issue Summary

Multiple customers reported that the analytics dashboard remained stuck on the loading screen after logging in.

## Priority

High

## Status

In Progress

## Customer Impact

Users could not access business reports, analytics, or export data required for daily operations.

## Investigation

- Verified customer accounts were active.
- Reproduced the issue successfully.
- Confirmed login functionality worked normally.
- Identified timeout responses from the Reports API.
- Escalated the incident to the engineering team.

## Resolution Plan

- Engineering to investigate backend API performance.
- Monitor service health.
- Notify customers once service is restored.
